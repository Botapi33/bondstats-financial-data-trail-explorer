Why lineage matters

From source to signal

Financial data is often copied, normalized, combined and reused before it reaches a chart or signal. Lineage makes that chain inspectable: origin, transformation, dependency and downstream use become part of the data itself.

BondStats approach

Transparency without pretending

This first version maps known source and product relationships. It deliberately avoids inventing update timestamps, observations or quality scores. Live operational metadata can be attached later where a source or pipeline exposes it reliably.
